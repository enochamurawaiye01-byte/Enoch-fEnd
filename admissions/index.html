Before you apply

A little preparation
can help.

Parents and guardians may wish to have the applicant’s basic contact information, previous school details, and guardian details ready before starting the form. The school can advise on any additional requirements after a submission is received.

Already have an account?

Students, teachers, parents, and staff with an existing account can continue through the school’s sign-in page. Parents and guardians who need an account can use the parent registration page.

  • Student, teacher, or staff sign-in: use the school login page.
  • Parent / guardian registration: use the existing parent application form.
  • Questions about admissions: contact the school by telephone or email for the latest information.
