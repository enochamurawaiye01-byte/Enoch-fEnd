Before you apply

A little preparation
can help.

You may wish to have the applicant’s basic contact and academic information available while completing the online form. The school will advise you if any additional information is needed. Contact admissions to confirm current requirements rather than relying on an assumed document list.

Already have an account?

Students, teachers, parents, and staff with an existing account can continue through the school’s central sign-in page. Parents or guardians who need an account can use the existing parent registration page.

  • Student, teacher, or staff sign-in: use the school login page.
  • Parent / guardian registration: use the existing parent application.
  • Questions about admissions: contact the school by telephone or email.
